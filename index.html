🦊 METAMASK · CRONOS RPC REPAIR GUIDE
RPC 0X19 CUSTOM ERROR FIX CRONOS MAINNET (CHAIN 25) UPDATE IN METAMASK SETTINGS
WHY DID THIS ERROR OCCUR?
Your MetaMask (or Web3 wallet) is currently configured with a custom RPC URL for Cronos Mainnet (Chain ID 25 / 0x19) that is rate-limited or throwing an HTTP client error when your wallet calls eth_getBlockByNumber ("RPC 0x19 Custom").
HOW TO FIX IN METAMASK (TAKES 10 SECONDS):
  1. Open MetaMask and open the network selector (top-left dropdown).
  2. Click the Settings (Gear) icon next to Cronos Mainnet, OR go to Settings → Networks → Cronos Mainnet.
  3. Replace your current New RPC URL with one of these reliable public endpoints:
    • https://cronos-evm.publicnode.com
    • https://1rpc.io/cro
    • https://cronos.drpc.org
  4. Click Save! Your transactions will immediately succeed!
